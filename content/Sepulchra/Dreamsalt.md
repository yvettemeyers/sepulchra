Dreamsalt is a powerful crystalline substance which is used to create Dreamglass, which can contain magical spells to keep them ongoing without requiring the focus of a spellcaster. This immense power directly led to the rise of the new political superpower [[Tenoxchi]] to directly compete with their previously unmatched rivals in [[Hyperium]]. Though it can be broken down into fine powder, it is measured by weight in "spell units" based on how much Dreamsalt is needed to improve a spell or create a small Dreamglass crystal.
