[[Mr. Magic]] discovered that there was a hole from underneath the camelphant carriage where someone was able to steal the cargo from underneath without being seen through a false bottom in the display case. [[Sable]] was able to sniff out that it was a [[Mankin]]. The party then followed the tracks to a small village named Orissi with a bazaar for travelers passing through towards [[Oristje]]. 

In town, [[Beebo]] and Sable were able to convince the [[Hyperium|Hyperian]] sheriff Gribby that they were sent by the imperial office to supplement his law enforcement contingent, as one deputy had been lost in the desert of [[The Red Wastes]], and the rest were mainly getting drunk at The Watering Hole. They both were given deputy badges as well as an old arquebus with one cartridge left.

Mr. Magic and Kahlia discovered that there was to be a deal later that night between a gang headed by [[Ventrix]] and a gang headed by [[Charles Cheese]], where Mr. Cheese was going to buy a giant red crystal from Ventrix's gang. [[M.W. Dot]] convinced Mr. Cheese that they were a powerful criminal in their own right by staging them fighting deputies in town and escaping (but the deputies were just Beebo and Sable.)

The party met up with Charles Cheese and a few of his goons to discuss potentially overthrowing Ventrix and stealing the score without paying. He agreed to do a hostile takeover and they all met at the entrance of a quarry mine. Once Ventrix and his goons arrived, they all aimed arquebuses and swords at each other, Dot raised a large mother camelphant skeleton halfway out of the sand at the bottom of the quarry, Kahlia threw a smoke bomb, Mr. Magic set up a throwing knife tripwire at the entrance into the mines, and a shot rang out that was obscured by the smoke.
